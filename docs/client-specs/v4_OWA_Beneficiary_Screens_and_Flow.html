How the flow must work

ConditionOutcome
All primaries surviveOriginal selected shares apply.
Some primaries surviveSurvivors receive the residue in proportion to their original shares. A/B remain inactive.
One primary survivesThat primary receives 100%.
No primary survivesOne shared substitute group applies: A 100%, or A/B divided to total 100%.
Substitute group active; A fails to survive or take a vested interestPreserve A’s source clause passing A’s share to A’s surviving children equally. Do not copy this clause to B.

Screen states to implement

  1. Initial primary: one empty person selection, share 100%.
  2. Multiple primary: add second/third without silently changing existing percentages; show remaining or overallocated amount.
  3. Validation: no continuation without complete selected profiles and positive shares totalling 100%.
  4. Substitute A: one shared group, A at 100%; optional add B.
  5. A/B split: positive percentages totalling 100%, independently from the primary group.
  6. Review: separate tables, separate totals, edit actions and conditional explanations.

Shared person editor

“Add a new person” opens the existing person editor: full English/Arabic name, DOB, nationality, passport number and upload, applicable Emirates ID number and upload, compulsory address, optional phone/email. Proof of address is not required for beneficiaries. Reuse documents already uploaded for another role. In this prototype only, the editor is represented by a labelled name-entry prompt; no real identity data should be entered.

Validation and navigation

  • Maximum three primary appointments and two substitute appointments. Block duplicate people within a group and the testator as beneficiary. Proposed safeguard: disallow a primary being their own group’s substitute.
  • Store percentages as fixed-point values. Each group totals 100%; never show a combined 200%.
  • Back preserves values. Production autosaves; this prototype only keeps in-memory state.
  • Removing an entry does not delete its person profile. Show any resulting share correction clearly; adding entries does not rebalance.
  • A is required in the documented standard flow; this remains an implementation assumption because skipping the entire group was not explicitly approved. B is optional.
  • No intake question asks who has died. Survival conditions belong to the Will; examples below are developer tests.

Template and data mapping

One primary group and one substitute group per Will; no per-primary substitute linkage. Retain A’s children fallback, the source trust-age 21 and related provisions. Adapt the singular primary condition to mean that none of the selected primaries survives. Preserve Section Seven’s undisposed-share provision with reviewed wording consistent with the intended order. Do not invent an outcome where B fails, A has no surviving children or all routes fail. English/Arabic clause review remains required.

Couple copying must retain both groups, apply the agreed partner swap and validate selections independently. Edits invalidate prior draft confirmation. Existing flat five-primary applications must not be silently converted.

Acceptance checklist

Test one/three primaries; under/over 100%; zero/negative shares; duplicate selection; A-only/A+B; adding/removing and Back; long names on mobile; missing documents; independent couple allocations; separate review totals; A’s fallback in both languages and no copied fallback for B. The final “continue” action goes to the main questionnaire review, not directly to payment.
